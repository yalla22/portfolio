# High-Reply-Probability Job Channels — Yashwanth

LinkedIn Easy Apply = lowest reply rate (you're 1 of 500+, often auto-screened). Spend your effort where a **human actually reads your application**. Ranked by reply probability:

```
HIGHEST  ┌─ 1. Referral (insider submits you)            → 5–10x callback rate
         ├─ 2. Direct company careers page + email HM    → high, you're in their own pipeline
         ├─ 3. Direct-to-founder platforms (Wellfound..) → founders read these
         ├─ 4. Niche/referral job boards                 → relevant, low noise
LOWEST   └─ 5. LinkedIn / Naukri mass "Easy Apply"        → black hole, do last
```

## Weekly routine (reply-optimized, LinkedIn last)
- **Mon:** Pull fresh openings (career pages of the niche list + Wellfound/Cutshort/Instahyre). Shortlist 15–20.
- **Tue–Thu:** For each → (a) find a referral via GetMeReferred/alumni, (b) apply on the **company site**, (c) **email the hiring manager/careers inbox** with the tailored resume + 3-line note.
- **Fri:** Follow up on anything from 5+ days ago (a polite nudge doubles reply rates).
- LinkedIn: only for *finding people to ask for referrals*, not for Easy Apply.

**Target: 15–20 referral-backed / direct applications per week.** Fewer, higher-quality, far better reply rate than 100 LinkedIn blasts.

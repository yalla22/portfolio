## 0) The strategy in one line
Apply *broadly* to high-volume CV/Backend roles for sheer numbers, but spend your *best energy* on the small niche of drone/geospatial-AI companies where you're a near-perfect match and competition is thin. Referrals + recruiter DMs beat "apply" buttons every time.

**Priority order for fastest replies:**
1. **Drone / Geospatial-AI companies** (niche — highest match, least competition) → use the *ML/CV* or *Drone/Robotics* resume.
2. **Computer Vision / ML Engineer** roles at product companies → *ML/CV* resume.
3. **Backend / Python (FastAPI)** roles → *Backend* resume (largest volume = most shots).
4. **Full-Stack** roles → *Full-Stack* resume (fill-in volume).

6. Set **"Immediately available / Immediate joiner"** in your headline/summary.

## 6) Daily routine to land interviews fast (≈1.5 hrs/day)
- **15 min** — check Naukri/LinkedIn/Instahyre alerts; apply to 5–10 fresh, relevant postings (use the matching resume).
- **30 min** — send 5 recruiter/hiring-manager LinkedIn DMs for roles you applied to.
- **20 min** — send 2–3 referral asks to 2nd-degree connections at target companies.
- **15 min** — apply directly on 1–2 niche drone/geospatial company career pages.
- **10 min** — log everything in a tracker (company, role, date, resume version, contact, status).

**Target: 50+ quality applications + 25+ direct messages per week.** In a market this size, that volume + your niche reliably produces interviews.
